# Stockout Escalation SOP

## Trigger
Top-seller available inventory reaches **0**, or projected days-of-cover falls below 2 for a high-velocity SKU.

## Immediate actions
1. Confirm stockout in `inventory_snapshots` for the SKU (example: Wireless Earbuds Pro / `SKU-1001`).
2. Quantify cancelled / lost orders during the stockout window.
3. Freeze paid campaigns featuring the stocked-out SKU.
4. Expedite replenishment PO and communicate ETA to CX.

## Stockout escalation path
- Hour 0-4: Ops lead + inventory planner notified.
- Hour 4-24: Merchandising pauses ads; CX macros updated.
- Day 2+: Leadership review if recovery ETA slips.

## Evidence to attach
Inventory time series, cancelled order totals, and SKU revenue mix before/after stockout.

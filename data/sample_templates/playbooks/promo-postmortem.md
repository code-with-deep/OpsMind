# Promo Post-Mortem Checklist

## When to run
After a paid campaign ends, or when revenue/mix shifts during an active flash sale.

## Checklist
1. Identify campaign name, channel, discount, featured SKU, and dates.
2. Measure featured SKU lift vs non-featured SKU softness (cannibalization).
3. Example pattern: **Charger Flash Sale** on `SAMPLE-1004` can pull share from other accessories.
4. Compare contribution margin, not just top-line units.
5. Decide: extend, reshape creative, or stop if halo is negative.

## Cannibalization signals
- Featured SKU revenue up while total category revenue flat/down.
- High-AOV hero SKU share declines during the promo window.
- Return rates unchanged (rules out quality confounders).
